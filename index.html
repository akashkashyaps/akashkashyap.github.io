Side projects with big plans

Cantonment 1946: Kere

A detective game set in Bangalore in July 1946, a year before Independence. The city is split between the Mysore State city and the British Cantonment, each with its own police. A surveyor is found dead in the dry bed of Sampangi tank, right on the line between them.

You play Ravi, a studio photographer hired to find out what happened. Shoot the evidence on a limited stock of glass plates, develop them in your darkroom, question suspects who all have something to hide, and piece the night together on your timeline board.

ROBIN

A live hologram you can talk to, and it answers in real time. We showed it to 100+ visitors at the Nottingham Trent University open day, with 95% positive feedback.

We layered our own software noise cancellation on top of the mic's, so it could hear one speaker clearly in a noisy hall and pick out who was talking when several people spoke at once. Answers come from RAG on local Ollama models, with Audio2Face animating a MetaHuman in Unreal Engine.

HistoriChat

A chatbot that answers questions about history. I tested whether it answers better when retrieval understands when things happened, not just what happened.

Standard RAG compared with time-aware RAG on 916 synthetic Q&A pairs made with RAGAS, plus a GraphRAG version on Neo4j. Runs locally with Ollama and Docker.
